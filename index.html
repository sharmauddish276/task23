Greenwood Public Library is a welcoming space for readers, students and families. Our collection offers something for everyone, from fiction to educational books. Visitors can explore new interests and enjoy a comfortable environment for reading.

We believe reading creates opportunities. Our library provides educational resources, comfortable reading spaces and community activities for people of all ages. We encourage curiosity, creativity and lifelong learning.
